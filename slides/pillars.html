THE LANGUAGE SURFACE
Six pillars for agent engineering
The framework turns operational expectations into constructs developers can review, test, and enforce.
01
Intent-first syntax
Goals, constraints, and success criteria are explicit before execution.
02
Typed tool contracts
External actions carry schemas, timeouts, retries, and capability scope.
03
Policy-native execution
Permissions, approvals, privacy rules, and budgets are runtime obligations.
04
Evidence-bound memory
Observations and inferences remain separated, scoped, and traceable.
05
Evaluation loops
Plans, tool use, grounding, latency, cost, and approvals become regression targets.
06
Deployment observability
Traces, lineage, receipts, and audit events are emitted by default.
