LOW-DEGREE TESTING
What FRI contributes
Phase 1: Commit & Fold
Recursive Folding
The prover reduces a large polynomial claim into two claims of half the size, merged using verifier challenges.
Merkle Commitments
Each folded layer is committed via a Merkle root. This continues until the polynomial reaches a trivial constant degree.
The Proximity Guarantee
FRI proves that the committed evaluation vector is close to a low-degree Reed-Solomon codeword, preventing "hybrid codeword" attacks.
Phase 2: Query & Verify
Selective Openings
The verifier queries random positions in the evaluation domain, receiving values and their Merkle authentication paths.
Colinearity Checks
The verifier checks that the queried points across layers satisfy the algebraic folding relation derived from the challenges.
Transition Validity
By proving the low-degree property of the composition polynomial, FRI establishes that the AIR constraints hold across the trace.
