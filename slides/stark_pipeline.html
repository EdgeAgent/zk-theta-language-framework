PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE
COMPILER & RUNTIME PIPELINE
STARK-style IOP/FRI Integration
01
Theta Source to IR
Compile .theta into a typed agent IR with explicit event tags and state transitions.
02
AIR Constraint Generation
Lower IR into Algebraic Intermediate Representation (AIR) transition and boundary constraints.
03
Execution Trace
Runtime records a witness trace of agent cycles, tool results, and policy evaluations.
04
Composition Polynomial
Combine constraints into a single composition polynomial for low-degree testing.
05
Merkle Commitments
Commit to evaluation vectors of trace and composition polynomials via Merkle roots.
06
Fiat-Shamir Transcript
Absorb commitments and public inputs to derive non-interactive verifier challenges.
07
FRI Proximity Queries
Recursively fold codewords and open Merkle paths to prove low-degree proximity.
08
Proof Receipt
Verify commitments, paths, and folding to issue a signed, observable proof receipt.
